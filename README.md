Financial Services Complaint Intelligence & Risk Analysis

Business Problem
Financial institutions receive complaints across multiple products, issues, and customer-service channels. Management needs to understand where complaint volumes are concentrated, which issues create the greatest operational risk, and where response performance requires improvement.

The objective of this project was to turn consumer complaint data into actionable insights that can help Customer Operations, Product, and Risk & Compliance teams prioritize improvement efforts.

Analytical Approach
  1. Quantified complaint concentration across financial products, issues, companies, and time periods to identify the major sources of consumer complaints.  
  2. Diagnosed response-performance gaps by comparing companies, complaint issues, and submission channels based on timely-response performance.
  3. Prioritized product–issue risk areas using complaint volume and untimely-response rates, while benchmarking company response risk against product mix.

Key Business Recommendations
  1. Prioritize high-volume, high-untimely-response product–issue combinations for operational improvement.
  2. Target companies with higher-than-expected response risk after accounting for product mix, rather than judging performance solely on raw complaint counts.
  3. Monitor emerging complaint trends by tracking product and issue-level changes over time to identify areas requiring early intervention.
